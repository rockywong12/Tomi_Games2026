# Geo Blitz! — Game Guide ⚡
### Version 3 — Updated Edition

---

## Overview

**Geo Blitz!** is a fast-paced obstacle-dodging game inspired by Geometry Dash, designed for children and casual players. Run, jump, and fly your character through 20 handcrafted levels across 4 themed worlds — each level is now longer, faster, and packed with a harder second half that ramps up the challenge as you push toward the finish line!

---

## How to Play

**Controls**
- **Tap / Click** — jump, double-jump, or activate your character's special ability
- **Hold** — fly upward (Ship character only)
- **Pause button (⏸)** — tap the pause icon in the top-left corner to pause; tap again to resume
- **Mute button (🔊)** — tap the speaker icon to toggle music and sound effects on/off

**Goal:** Reach the end of each level without hitting any obstacles. The fewer times you die, the more stars you earn!

**Play in landscape mode.** If you see a "Rotate Your Device!" screen, turn your phone sideways.

## Characters

### ⬜ Cubey (Cube) ⭐ Popular
- **Ability:** Tap once to jump, tap twice to **double jump**
- **Feel:** Balanced weight — the classic Geo Blitz experience
- **Tip:** Save the second tap for the peak of the first jump to get maximum height

### 🚀 Shippy (Ship) ⭐ Popular
- **Ability:** Hold to fly up, release to fall
- **Feel:** Smooth and floaty — great for navigating ceiling gaps
- **Tip:** Short taps give fine control; holding too long will slam you into the ceiling

### 🔵 Bally (Ball) ⭐ Popular
- **Ability:** Tap to flip gravity — you fall upward!
- **Feel:** Unique and mind-bending; great for ceiling-spike sections
- **Tip:** Tap rhythmically to bounce between floor and ceiling

### ⭐ Starry (Star)
- **Ability:** Double tap for a floaty double jump
- **Feel:** Lighter than Cubey with a slower fall
- **Tip:** Use the slow fall to line up second jumps over wide gaps

### 💧 Droppy (Teardrop)
- **Ability:** Double tap for an extra-floaty double jump
- **Feel:** Very light gravity — drifts through the air
- **Tip:** Great for beginners; on fast levels the slow drift can make tight sections harder

### 🚀 Rockety (Rocket)
- **Ability:** Tap twice for a high-powered **turbo double jump**
- **Feel:** Rockets extremely high; fast fall
- **Tip:** Use sparingly — the turbo burst easily overshoots gaps

## Obstacle Types

**Ground Obstacles**

- **Single Spike (▲)** — Jump over it. The most basic obstacle.
- **Double Spike (▲▲)** — Two side-by-side spikes. Needs a precise jump.
- **Triple Spike (▲▲▲)** — Three spikes wide. Needs a high or double jump.
- **Block (■)** — Solid square. Jump on top of it or leap over entirely.
- **Tall Block (▬)** — Double-height block. Must jump over it.

**Aerial Obstacles**

- **Floating Block** — A block hovering mid-air at various heights. Duck under or jump over depending on position — look ahead!
- **Ceiling Spike (▼)** — Hangs down from the ceiling. Dangerous for Ship players and anyone near the top.
- **Double Ceiling Spike (▼▼)** — Two ceiling spikes together. Keep low!
- **Circle** — A round enemy floating at different heights with cartoon eyes. Dodge around it.

## Tips & Tricks

- **The "Second Half" rule:** If you reach the midpoint of a level, expect significantly harder patterns ahead — don't let your guard down.
- **Double Jump timing:** Your second jump resets on landing. Don't use it too early or you'll have nothing left for the harder obstacles.
- **Watch the progress bar:** The 🏁 flag shows how close you are to finishing.
- **Floating blocks and ceiling spikes together** are the hardest combos — there's a narrow window to get through. Commit early.
- **Ship players:** At World 3 speeds, the ship becomes very sensitive. Use micro-taps for fine altitude control.
- **World 4 speeds (8.3–9.6)** leave less than half a second to react at minimum obstacle spacing — muscle memory from earlier worlds helps enormously.
