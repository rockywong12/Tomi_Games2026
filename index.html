COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Math: The Elements really free?
Yes — 100% free, forever. No hidden fees, no premium tiers, no ads. Just open the game and play.
Do I need to create an account?
No account needed at all. Just visit the page and start playing immediately. No email, no password, no sign-up.
What devices does the game work on?
Math: The Elements works on any device with a modern browser — smartphones, tablets, Chromebooks, laptops, and desktop computers. No app download needed.
Is the game safe for children?
Absolutely. The game contains no ads, no external links, no social features, and collects no personal data from players. It is a fully self-contained educational game.
How many questions are in the game?
There are over 900 unique math questions across all grade levels. Each grade (3–7) has 5 worlds with 30 questions each, covering different math topics per world.
Can teachers use this in the classroom?
Yes! Math: The Elements is perfect for classroom use — no setup, no accounts, works on any school device. Students just open the URL and start playing. Great as a math center activity or reward game.
What math topics does the game cover?
The game covers arithmetic, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, percentages, algebra basics, geometry (area, perimeter, volume), statistics, probability, coordinate planes, ratios, and more — matched to each grade level.
